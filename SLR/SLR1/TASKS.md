## Direct-source pilots and guidelines

After `S-001`, pilot each current interface without screening results. Apply the [two-mode validation policy](searches/two-mode-validation-policy.md): first use agent-driven exploration to make the current interface work, then use a separate frozen deterministic evidence attempt; broad offline suites are not routine launch prerequisites, and exploration findings do not by themselves pass acceptance gates. Produce the exact source-native translations, fields, controls, date and language handling, expansion behavior, pagination or export method, stable-identity and completeness checks, limits, and failure rules. Use the `S-D01`–`S-D04` interface work to complete `S-C01` before accepting those four guidelines. Store the guideline, translations, and diagnostic evidence in the assigned source packet; index accepted outputs from `search-log.md` and Part 2.

Each pilot must establish whether the source offers a genuine controlled vocabulary or distinct indexed-term field. Where it does, inspect scope notes, hierarchies and narrower terms, and descriptors assigned to known relevant records; test descriptors as OR supplements to the matching free-text block, without treating a generic `systems engineering` heading alone as MBSE. Record explicitly when no usable controlled vocabulary is available.
