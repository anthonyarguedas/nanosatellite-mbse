T2 replaces the bibliographic B2 R block and is tag-only where local core structurally contains it.
2. **NTRS population compression (moderate recall issue) — resolved prospectively.** The seven-form draft lacked a source-semantic equivalence justification for `nanosatellites`, `"nano satellite"`, and `"nano-satellites"`. All ten source-representable P forms are now explicit. The mandatory matrix is 180 P×M + 250 P×G + 230 P×C1 = **660 cells**; T2 remains local. A focused 198-cell no-screening pilot subsequently reconciled every remaining cell with valid zeros, but its yield cannot justify omission.
3. **ESA route-regression visibility (moderate reproducibility issue) — resolved.** The 19-case base matcher suite and separate 13-case route/individual-attachment aggregation suite, fixtures, result, and driver are tracked and explicitly linked. Route labels in the base fixture are not represented as aggregation proof.
