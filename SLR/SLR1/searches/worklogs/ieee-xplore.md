## Source limitations and handoff risks

- IEEE’s direct endpoint is an unfielded metadata search. It does not expose a field-by-field explanation of why each record matched, and many returned abstracts are truncated. Later screeners must not infer a qualifying EPS anchor, model anchor, bridge, RQ2 claim, or RQ3 V&V record from these metadata alone.
- Some broad-list records visibly appear unrelated or are document-level artifacts (for example, a table of contents). They are candidates for later protocol-based screening, not silently removed in this IEEE search slice.
- The endpoint provides metadata only; no PDFs, full texts, access checks, or open-access filtering were performed. `accessType` metadata was not used as a restriction.
- The search date is 2026-08-06. If the overall review’s final search date occurs later, an IEEE update search is needed to close the evidence window.
- No Book record was returned despite the required Books facet. This is a source-result observation, not a change in eligible publication types.
- IEEE-only deduplication is provisional. The integration agent must preserve all final query provenance while merging with Scopus and later supplementary/citation routes.

## Stopping rationale

- All four planned IEEE primary pilots (I-A1, I-A2, I-B1, I-B2) completed successfully, with full records retrieved and read.
- Both planned outcome/V&V sensitivity probes completed successfully and were fully redundant; outcome or V&V terms were never imposed on either baseline stream.
- Both Stream A and the deliberately EPS-free Stream B were retained. Stream A tested explicit EPS, functional/analysis, budget-expression, and component terminology; Stream B tested explicit MBSE/language, structured-model/engineering-concern, population spelling, and object-oriented-model sensitivities.
- No query exceeded the plan’s manageable 30–70-hit heuristic; the largest total was 41. No run was silently truncated: every nonzero full query was retrieved completely in one 100-record page.
- The last refinement wave was redundant or zero-result (A3, A5/A6, B3/B4), and the two required sensitivity probes added no new identity. Additional IEEE keyword variations are therefore more likely to be redundant/noisy than to improve this primary-database slice.
- Remaining recall risk is appropriately handed to the separately delegated Scopus search, subsequent supplementary discovery, full-text screening of broad Stream B candidates, and protocol-required citation chasing. No protocol change is required by this IEEE slice.
