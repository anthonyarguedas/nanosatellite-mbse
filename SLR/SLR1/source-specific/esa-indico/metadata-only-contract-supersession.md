# ESA metadata-only contract supersession

The metadata contract was introduced in browser adapter v8. The current v9 adapter keeps that category 0 harvest and adds mandatory search and result completion screenshots. It requires two matching root snapshots, every event export, a full metadata ledger, and its terminal marker. It does not fetch attachment bodies during search.

Pilot 003 and Pilot 005 content requirements describe historical adapter behavior. The prior attachment-content requirement is superseded by the metadata-only contract. Historical pilot evidence remains unchanged. It cannot establish current source coverage, completion, or zero results.

The full ledger retains normalized Event, Contribution, Subcontribution, and Material metadata. It includes unmatched attachment metadata and declared access state. Existing evidence remains bound to its v8 marker. New runs use v9 and require both screenshot receipts.

Pilot 006 completed this contract for exact A1 on 2026-09-02. Its two root snapshots each contained the same 440 ordered event IDs. The ledger contains 16,517 normalized metadata records and A1 matched zero records.

Pilot 007 executed all nine current expressions over that fixed marker-bound ledger. Their union contained 24 metadata records. The derivative pilot receipts remain nonmergeable. They validate the schedule on the fixed corpus and do not refresh the source or complete a final search.
