# Systematic Literature Review Planning

Based on the distinction made by [Peters et al.](https://journals.lww.com/ijebh/fulltext/2015/09000/guidance_for_conducting_systematic_scoping_reviews.5.aspx),
this is Systematic Literature Review (SLR) because it seeks to synthetize findings,
not just report them.
Also, it is classified as an SLR because it incorporates quality assessment.

Results presentation will follow the bibliometric analysis proposed by [Carrera-Rivera et al.](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2215016122002746), incorporating the insight from Peters et al. to group the results by PICOC elements.

## Quality Assessment Checklist

Both questions use the scale:
- No = 0
- Insufficiently = 0.5
- Yes = 1

- Was the research published in a credible venue?
- Do the researchers discuss any problems (limitations, threats) with the validity of their results?

Intentionally kept short to increase the breadth of the search.

## Data Extraction Form

- Method and Metrics
