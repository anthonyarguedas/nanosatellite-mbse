\section{Concern contributions to measures of effectiveness}
\label{appendix:framework-moe-concerns}

Table~\ref{tab:framework-moe-concerns} lists every contributing concern for each MOE defined in Table~\ref{tab:framework-lifecycle-moes}. Each row names all IDs in its group, including descendants. The preceding hierarchy preserves the parent concerns and alias references. Alias occurrences retain their IDs and share observations with their referenced concerns. Each contribution explains a reason to evaluate the MOE. It does not assert satisfaction of the concern.

Contributions cover EPS models and declared host software. Physical campaigns, replica maintenance, operational troubleshooting and target qualification remain outside scope. MOE-06 measures whether these exclusions and external evidence gaps are clear to reviewers. It does not measure completion of excluded work. MOE-05 records financial and access burden separately from person-hours. Targets require stakeholder agreement. No achieved values are reported.
