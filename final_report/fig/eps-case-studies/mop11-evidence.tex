\subsection{FR-11: catalogue recommendations from mission budgets}
\label{appendix:cots-case-results}

The reference calculations use the original budgets and manufacturer sheets independently of the catalogue, with 50 significant digits of decimal arithmetic.
Every query screens one purchased cell or single-cell solar cell assembly (SCA) against its directly owned requirements.
Installation counts remain separate.
For load energy $E_L$ in Wh, the required cell capacity and power are
\begin{equation*}
C_{\mathrm{req}}=\frac{E_L}{N_{\mathrm{bat}}V_{\mathrm{budget}}d\eta_d},
\qquad
P_{\mathrm{req}}=\frac{E_L/\eta_c+E_D}{N_{\mathrm{PV}}fT_s}.
\end{equation*}
Here $N_{\mathrm{bat}}$ and $N_{\mathrm{PV}}$ count installed battery and solar cells, $d$ is DoD, $\eta_d$ is battery delivery efficiency, $\eta_c$ is conversion efficiency, $E_D$ is diode loss in Wh, $f$ is mean sunlight projection and $T_s$ is sunlight duration in hours.
Capacity comparisons use nominal voltage as an energy estimate.
