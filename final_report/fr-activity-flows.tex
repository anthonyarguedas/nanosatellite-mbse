\small
These panels propose a decomposition of the 46 FRs in Table~\ref{tab:framework-requirements}. They add no requirements. Table~\ref{tab:fr-activity-coverage} indexes the visible activities. Appendix~\ref{appendix:framework-io} defines their inputs and outputs.

Short verb phrases name activities. Each header gives a panel/activity identifier and its existing FR IDs. Rectangles also identify retained records. Gray identifies external participants. Solid arrows carry information, dotted arrows carry findings or feedback, and dashed arrows carry authorization. Labels name the exchanged information. Brackets identify branch conditions. Arrows may carry an accumulated analysis basis. They prescribe no numerical algorithm. Transfer admission, assistance currency, result provenance and evidence controls apply wherever their FRs apply. Suggestions, execution and retained records confer no engineering approval. Engineers control engineering decisions. The designated review authority controls technical review acceptance. Physical execution and target hardware qualification are excluded.
