& Objective & Deliverable & Metric \\ \hline
1 & Characterize needs, constraints, interfaces and technical criteria of nanosatellite EPS. & Traceable matrix of EPS characterization, consolidating needs, constraints, interfaces, and technical criteria, indicating source or justification and relation to elements covered by the framework. & 100\% of characterized elements have a traceable source or justification and a classification \\
2 & Define the modeling, analysis, and evidence management workflow of the framework via evaluation and justified selection of MBSE methods, languages, and tools. & Defined and justified MBSE workflow, including phases with activities, inputs, outputs, tools, interfaces, and mechanisms to preserve the traceability and reproducibility of the evidence. & 100\% of workflow phases document evaluated alternatives and a decision, including selection criteria and justification. \\
3 & Model a generic EPS architecture with functions, logical components, interfaces, operation modes, and constraints. & Generic EPS architecture MBSE model, mission-independent and traceable to selected requirements. & 100\% of selected requirements are linked to one or more architecture elements, and the generic model coverage is contrasted with two case studies, one traditional based on BIRDS-4 and one high-autonomy case. \\
4 & Characterize early EPS requirement verification within the MBSE framework. & Early verification matrix, constituted by selected requirements, verification methods, pass/fail criteria, required evidence, and mechanisms for result management. & 100\% of requirements selected for verification report a verification means, pass/fail criteria, and associated evidence, maintaining traceability from requirement to verification means to result. \\
5 & Evaluate the MBSE framework in two contrasting EPS case studies & Evaluation report of the framework based on two contrasting case studies, one traditional based on BIRDS-4 and another high-autonomy case, including acceptance and performance results. & The two case studies are completed using the same framework core, with traceable and reproducible evidence and without post hoc modifications to the framework. The acceptance and performance metrics used for evaluation are reported. \\
