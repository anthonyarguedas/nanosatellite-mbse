\section{Optimize and Evaluate Alternatives}
\label{section:optimize-and-evaluate-alternatives}
\label{sec:framework-trade-review}

This report recommends PA-VS under the analyst's declared priority to reuse documented services before developing replacements. The rationale and conditions are given below. This is a conditional design direction, not a procurement decision, measured ranking or established global optimum. OOSEM requires a common analysis context linked to stakeholder measures \citep[Secs.~8.11 and~17.3.6]{friedenthal2014practical}.

\subsection{Common comparison basis, eligibility criteria and evidence}

Compare the complete minimum configurations, including all additions and dependencies, on the same cases, tasks, references and supported profiles. Pin models/solvers to isolate overhead, then assess native end-to-end work. Include manual steps, access, maintenance and recovery. Scenes earn credit only through activated decision-specific validation. CPU controller SIL and supplied-data replay remain required. HIL needs later equipment, timing and safety evidence.

Six mandatory eligibility criteria precede empirical ranking: EG-01 named coverage for every mandatory requirement, EG-02 consistent information exchange and correct change processing, EG-03 credible and safe handling of limitations, EG-04 independent reconstruction from retained records, EG-05 reuse of a fixed common core in two cases, and EG-06 legal and deployable access, versions, exports and recovery. Each criterion must be satisfied. Favorable results on other criteria cannot compensate for a failure. All five concepts have compatible proposed realizations and unresolved checks. None has demonstrated compliance with these criteria in an implementation. Critical findings prevent affected technical assertions from being reported as fully supported. Incorrect tasks receive no favorable effort or cost assessment.

\subsection{Selection and reversal conditions}

The priority favors reuse of documented requirement, architecture, engineering-value and procedure services before recreating general services. PA-VS has the strongest combined source basis for that priority \citep[Secs.~2.1 and~3.3--3.4]{citation-4690}\citep[Secs.~3.1 and~4.3--4.4]{campioli2025integration}. This addresses expanded manager, systems and technician concerns. It proves no lower total effort, cost or error rate. Complete model, RAM, software, safety and replay services remain substantial development.

Reopen selection if access/export/recovery fails, stable-ID transactions fail, or synchronization removes reuse benefit. PA-CDT's CPU core is the principal alternative under local semantic authority and portable release priorities, conditional on its native quantity, FMI and replay checks. The other three remain admissible if complete common-task evidence supports them. First compare requirement/procedure work, a coupled subsystem/BOM/software change and clean replay after working-data changes. Continue all planned V verification procedures and MOE evaluation procedures. Report raw outcomes and uncertainty before identifying a Pareto set or applying preference-based utility functions. A Pareto set contains candidates for which no alternative is at least as good on every criterion and better on one. The empirical Pareto set is undetermined. Any later score needs explicit preferences, reference values and sensitivity analysis. Avoid double counting. Literature frequencies and evidence categories do not justify numerical importance weights.

\subsection{Selected physical scope and component obligations}
\label{section:selected-physical-architecture}

PA-VS separates four responsibilities, which may share computing resources:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Engineering and review} (\path{VS-LN-ENG}): requirements, component choices, BOM/cost information, guidance and stakeholder reviews in the Valispace workspace.
\item \textbf{Architecture and interfaces} (\path{VS-LN-ARCH}): EPS structure, function allocations, modes and interfaces in Capella, linked to common flight/ground software definitions.
\item \textbf{Analysis and verification} (\path{VS-LN-ANALYSIS}): dynamic EPS and battery analysis, RAM, model credibility, software checks and verification proposals.
\item \textbf{Configuration and evidence assurance} (\path{VS-LN-ASSURANCE}): controlled information exchanges, accepted revisions, retained evidence and independent reconstruction.
\end{enumerate}
Generic libraries, adapters and methods are framework assets. Mission-specific models, hardware and software designs are controlled case artifacts.

In this proposed realization, Capella maintains structure, allocations and mode transitions. Valispace maintains requirements, criteria references, component characteristics, quantities, quotes and budget formulas. Shared software definitions and identified analysis-model revisions avoid competing copies. Released baselines retain the native design revisions and raw evidence needed to reconstruct a decision. Transfers require stable identities, previewed differences, authorized updates and recovery after conflicts or interruptions. Matching names alone is insufficient. Battery-aging and controller-model adequacy still require case-specific development and evidence.

The engineering workstation (VS-HOST-ENG) hosts Capella, in-application Python, the controlled exchange and proposed local OpenModelica analysis. The vendor service (VS-HOST-VALI) holds working engineering data. The managed store (VS-HOST-STORE) retains released evidence, with backup arrangements to confirm. Discipline services (VS-HOST-SPECIALIST) and software-build/target evidence (VS-HOST-FLIGHTBUILD) remain external dependencies. Versions, mappings, access and capacity are unresolved. The minimum configuration requires no GPU, EPS replica or real-time target. Start with the reported in-application path \citep[Sec.~3.3]{citation-4690}. Headless export would require separate verification.

The systems engineer stewards requirements and releases, the EPS engineer and project manager steward component/cost information, and qualified contributors review models and software. EPS engineers and technicians review verification proposals. Physical execution remains subject to facility authorization.
